The people behind MolarBear

Meet the people
behind better care.

Our team brings together experience across dentistry, hospitality, regulatory affairs, operations, growth strategy and patient advocacy. We share a belief that exceptional care depends not only on clinical outcomes, but also on how people feel understood, respected and supported throughout their journey.

What brings us together

Care should never end when the appointment does.

At MolarBear, we believe that better dental care is about more than appointments and treatment plans. It's about people, trust and the conversations that happen in between.

We're bringing together people who care about making healthcare experiences more thoughtful, connected and personal.

Our people

The team making it happen.

Different perspectives, shared purpose. Here's a little introduction to the people behind MolarBear.

Ishan Gupta

Co-founder

An engineer by training, Ishan spent five years running a hospitality business and has also worked in private equity. He enjoys exploring the intersection of business and culture. Coming from three generations of doctors, his biggest learning is that the practice of care is as much an art as it is a science.

Dr. Ratnakshi Kant

Co-founder

A dentist by training, Ratnakshi brings 12 years of regulatory experience across organisations including McKesson Canada, Sanofi, Genzyme, Roche and Biogen. She brings a deep understanding of compliance and the discipline required to build responsibly around medicine and patient care.

Avin Sethi

Head of Operations

Avin studied business administration at York University's Schulich School of Business in Toronto and has led operational excellence at Amazon and customer experience excellence at lululemon. Our operations wizard, Avin brings the structure, discipline and attention to detail that turn an ambitious service promise into dependable everyday delivery.

Raghav Upmanyu

GTM Strategist

Raghav brings over seven years of experience in growth, digital strategy and performance marketing, working across consumer brands and technology businesses. At MolarBear, he focuses on translating the vision into a clear go-to-market strategy — shaping how we position the service, reach dental practices and build meaningful relationships with the people we serve.

Sibel

Patient Advocacy Advisor

Sibel brings intellectual clarity, emotional insight and a deep commitment to patient advocacy. Our moral compass, he helps us keep patients' experiences, voices and choices at the centre of what we build.
