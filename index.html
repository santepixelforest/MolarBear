Research & context
  1. Henry Schein One, June 2026. Reports 45% average case acceptance and 75% for the top 10%, citing its 2026 Catalyst Index. Its article focuses on DSOs and multi-location practices. Acceptance includes scheduled or completed care; it is not the same as treatment completion. The benchmark gap is not an expected MolarBear uplift.
  2. Press Ganey, February 2025. Reports appointment barriers among 48.4% of healthcare consumers. This is broader healthcare evidence, not a dental-specific estimate or proof of what causes the acceptance gap.
  3. DeGroff et al., 2017. Randomized trial of patient navigation for colonoscopy completion within six months: 61.1% with navigation versus 53.2% with usual care (p = 0.021).
